# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Welcome people of different
backgrounds and experience levels. Disagreement about design is expected;
harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, and sharing
another person's private financial or identity data are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews,
and other project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject changes,
temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when needed
to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Reports
will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical.
